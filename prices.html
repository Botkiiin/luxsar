Why choose two-step polishing?

01
Cutting stage

A compound and cutting pad level or reduce visible wash swirls, oxidation and other defects that can safely be corrected. This stage removes a very small amount of clear coat.

02
Finishing stage

A finer polish and finishing pad refine the surface, remove haze or micro-marring left by the first stage and bring out a clearer, deeper gloss.

Not every scratch can be safely removed. We inspect the paint and, where appropriate, measure its thickness before recommending a correction level.
